OmaPen

Agent settings

Only agents that can run with all their tools switched off are accepted, because text from a web page is not trusted. ollama runs a model on your machine and has no tools.

Empty gives claude haiku, gives ollama your most recent pull, and leaves pi and omp on their own default.
